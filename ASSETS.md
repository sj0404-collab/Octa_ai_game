# Assets

**Art direction:** A dense semi-3D corporate office nightmare rendered as a sharp digital-glass stealth game. Indigo office geometry and cold blue infrastructure establish the night shift; Relay Mint `#60F6D4` is reserved for the worker’s agency, healing and exit; coral marks active corporate threat; amber identifies valuables, safes and risk. The tilted top-down camera must show walls, stair links, furniture and moving hazards without sacrificing touch readability.
